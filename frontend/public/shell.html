Fauci should be held in contempt of Congress for refusing to testify at the Senate Homeland Security Committee hearing. This is supported by: Fauci refused to answer every question from senators after being directed to testify. Fauci's Fifth Amendment privilege is unsupported because he holds an unlimited presidential pardon, which rests on the presidential pardon having given Fauci more immunity than most people get. It is against the law to obstruct an investigation of Congress. Congress held Lois Lerner in contempt after she gave an opening statement and then took the Fifth, on the ground that you cannot do both.

Against the main claim: the sole reason Paul called Fauci before the committee is to get him to say something that would vindicate Paul's public pledges that Fauci would end up behind bars. This rests on Paul having publicly released Fauci's unredacted personal diary, aimed at embarrassing and intimidating him, and on Paul having repeatedly and publicly pledged that Fauci would end up behind bars.

Against the pardon claim: Fauci has a valid Fifth Amendment privilege and the right to assert it. Invoking the Fifth Amendment is Fauci's constitutional right.

Against the main claim: the hearing legitimizes a predetermined conclusion Paul reached years ago, under the guise of a committee investigation. This rests on Paul having called in 2021 for Fauci to be fired and arrested, and on the committee having denied the minority access to the investigation's records.

Against the records claim: all of the investigation's records were posted on a website, and no Democrat ever asked for the access code.

Against the setup claim: the hearing's purpose was to get answers and an apology for why U.S. taxpayers funded dangerous research at a lab in China without adequate safety standards, which rests on NIAID having funded research in a lab in China that lacked acceptable safety standards.

Against the lab claim: the viruses studied under the NIH grant were molecularly so far removed from SARS-CoV-2 that they could not have turned into it.

Against that: Fauci privately doubted the natural-origin account while telling the public a lab leak was implausible.

Against that: Fauci always said natural origin was very highly likely, and once a lab leak was being discussed he said no one was certain and called for a thorough investigation.
