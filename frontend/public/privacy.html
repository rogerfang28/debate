Privacy

This site keeps as little as it can. Here is all of it.

What the site stores in your browser

One cookie, for the session. It tells the server which reader you are, so the page can keep your place as you move around. It is deleted when you close your browser.

One browser value. A random visitor name such as visitor-3f9a1c2b, kept in your browser's local storage so you come back as the same reader for up to two days. It is not tied to anything about you. Your browser also keeps a few display preferences, like whether a panel is folded.

Nothing here is used for analytics, advertising, or tracking across other sites, and nothing is shared with anyone. There are no third-party trackers on the site.

If you sign in

An account stores your username, a hashed password if you set one, and the claims, challenges and agreements you make. Those contributions are part of the debates they belong to and are visible to everyone who reads them.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells the site your email address, which becomes your username. The site does not receive your Google password and does not post anything to your Google account.

The AI chat

When you ask the chat a question, the text of the debate you are reading and your question are sent to the language model the site is configured to use, so it can answer. Your conversation with it is stored with your account so you can see it again, and you can clear it at any time from the chat.

Deleting your data

Clearing your browser's cookies and site data removes everything stored on your device. To delete an account and its contributions, use the contact on the site's home page; visitor identities that are never used again are removed automatically.
