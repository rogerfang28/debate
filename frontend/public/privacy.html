Privacy

This site keeps as little as it can. Here is all of it.

What the site stores in your browser

One cookie, for the session. It tells the server which reader you are, so the page can keep your place as you move around. It is deleted when you close your browser.

One browser value. A random visitor name such as visitor-3f9a1c2b, kept in your browser's local storage so you come back as the same reader for up to two days. It is not tied to anything about you, and the server deletes visitor identities after three days. Your browser also keeps a few display preferences, like whether a panel is folded.

Nothing here is used for analytics, advertising, or tracking across other sites, and nothing is shared with anyone. There are no third-party trackers on the site.

If you sign in

An account stores your username, the date you joined, a hashed password if you set one, and the claims, challenges and agreements you make. Those contributions are part of the debates they belong to and are visible to everyone who reads them.

If you sign in with Google, Google tells the site your email address and an id for your Google account. Your username is made from the part of the address before the @, and you can change it. The address itself is kept private. The site does not receive your Google password and does not post anything to your Google account.

Your profile

Anyone can see: your username, the month you joined, your statistics (debates, claims, challenges, agreements, concessions, and challenges waiting on you), and the debates and claims you take part in.

Nobody else ever sees: your email address, your password, or whether you sign in with Google.

The AI chat

When you ask the chat a question, the text of the debate you are reading and your question are sent to the language model the site is configured to use, so it can answer. Your conversation with it is stored with your account so you can see it again, and you can clear it at any time from the chat.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself: click your name at the top of the page, then Delete account on your profile. That erases your email, password, Google link, saved place on the site and AI chat history, and signs you out. The account can't be signed into again.

Your claims, challenges and agreements stay in their debates, because other people's arguments answer them. They show as "Deleted account".

Clearing your browser's cookies and site data removes everything stored on your device.
