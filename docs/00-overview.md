# Overview — What the Debate Tool Is

## One sentence

A **Structured Logical Debate Platform** that fights misinformation through *structural accountability*: instead of trusting a fact-checker's verdict, you can see the logical structure of an argument and verify the reasoning yourself.

## The problem

Existing fact-checking asks for blind trust. Two fact-checkers can disagree, sources conflict, and a reader has no way to inspect *why* a claim is considered true or false. Meanwhile, online arguments are dominated by dodging, topic changes, and ad hominem attacks — none of which are visible or penalized.

## The idea

Make the **logical structure of arguments visible and enforceable**:

- Claims are explicit nodes. Support and attacks are explicit links.
- If you challenge a claim, the other side must **respond or concede** — dodging is structurally impossible.
- Readers and judges can see, at a glance, which claims were defended and which were conceded.
- Over time, the platform accumulates a searchable database of rebuttals and evidence, so the same lie doesn't have to be debunked from scratch every time.

## Mission

> Make it **easiest to see the TRUTH** and **hardest to spread lies** — by structurally exposing holes in false claims so they can be pointed out and debunked.

## What it is *not*

- Not a fact-checker that hands down verdicts. The tool never asserts "true/false" on your behalf — it exposes structure so *you* can weigh it (see design case 1 in `11-design-cases.md`).
- Not a popularity contest. Agreement/upvotes are kept strictly separate from argumentative support (design case 9).
